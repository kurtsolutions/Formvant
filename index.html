Industrial Design, Product Design & Innovation

Studio

We shape ideas into meaningful objects. Combining design, engineering and innovation, we create products that connect functionality with timeless aesthetics.

FORMVANT is a fictional studio. Everything on this site is self-initiated concept work, created to explore how a product can feel precise, calm and necessary.

Manifesto

  • Function first. Every surface earns its place by doing something.
  • Beauty follows. Proportion, material and light, reduced to what remains essential.
  • Innovation, quietly. The best new idea feels like it was always there.

Expertise

  • Industrial Design

    Form, proportion and material for objects people live with.

  • Product Development

    From first sketch to a product ready to be made.

  • Concept Design

    Early directions that make an idea visible and testable.

  • Engineering

    Structure, mechanisms and tolerances that serve the design.

  • Prototyping

    Physical models to judge weight, touch and fit.

  • Design Strategy

    Clear positioning so the product has a reason to exist.
